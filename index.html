The person doing the work

Travis
Henriquez

I started Seeker Solutions because I was tired of watching two things happen in parallel: small businesses getting sold AI they didn't understand and couldn't use — and people losing their sense of purpose as the work they'd built their identity around started to disappear.

Those aren't separate problems. They're the same disruption seen from two angles. The businesses need clearer tools. The humans need clearer inner ground. I work on both, because I've had to work on both personally.

My background is genuinely mixed. I have a degree in Religious Studies with deep roots in Eastern traditions — Buddhism, Advaita Vedanta, Kashmir Shaivism, comparative mysticism. I've maintained a serious contemplative practice for years. I've also run kitchens, managed IT systems, and more recently built AI automation infrastructure for businesses. These things aren't in conflict for me. They've always felt like different expressions of the same underlying interest: how things actually work, and how to see them clearly.

I work remotely and take on a small number of clients at a time. If you're reading this and something is landing — reach out.
