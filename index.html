Travis Henriquez

I have a degree in philosophy and religious studies and more than 25 years of meditation practice rooted in Buddhism, Advaita Vedanta, and Kashmir Shaivism. I think of myself as a philosopher-mystic who spends his time at the edge of human thought, science, and wisdom.

I started Seeker Solutions for people like me: seekers. As AI takes over more of the work that gave people their identity and purpose, the old questions get urgent again. What am I for? What matters? What do I do now?

I also run kitchens, manage IT systems, and build AI automation, so I can help with the practical side too. I work remotely with a small number of people at a time.

The question isn’t whether AI will change your work. It will. The question is whether you’ll navigate that change consciously or get dragged through it.

Common questions

What is Seeker Solutions?

A solo practice founded by Travis Henriquez. It offers clarity and transition work for people finding meaning as AI changes work, and AI and automation consulting for the teachers, centers, and small organizations who serve them.

Is the Clarity Intensive therapy?

No. It is not therapy or manifestation coaching. It is three 75-minute sessions of direct, rigorous work on a specific question, drawing on religious studies, contemplative practice, and depth psychology.

What does the AI Audit include?

A recorded 60-minute discovery call, workflow mapping, a written report with prioritized recommendations, and a 30-minute follow-up, for a flat fee of $497.

What is the Seeker’s Path?

An upcoming membership of monthly inquiry and practice for people finding meaning in the machine age. Join the waitlist for founding-member access.
