About Seeker Solutions

Seeker Solutions exists for people asking the oldest questions in a new setting: what is worth doing, and who am I when the work changes? The practice draws on a degree in philosophy and religious studies and more than 25 years of contemplative practice in Buddhism, Advaita Vedanta, and Kashmir Shaivism.

As AI takes over more of the work that gave people their identity and purpose, those questions get urgent. What am I for? What matters? What do I do now?

The practical side matters too. Seeker Solutions also brings experience running hospitality operations and managing IT systems, and builds AI and automation for the teachers, centers, and small organizations who serve seekers.

Founded by Travis Henriquez.

Common questions

What is Seeker Solutions?

Clarity and transition work for people finding meaning as AI changes work, and AI and automation consulting for the teachers, centers, and small organizations who serve them.

What is the Threshold Assessment?

A free, eight-question reflection that helps you see where you are in a major transition and what kind of support might help. It is not a diagnosis or therapy.

Is the Clarity Intensive therapy?

No. It is not therapy or manifestation coaching. It is three 75-minute sessions of direct, rigorous work on a specific question, drawing on religious studies, contemplative practice, and depth psychology.

What does the AI Audit include?

A recorded 60-minute discovery call, workflow mapping, a written report with prioritized recommendations, and a 30-minute follow-up, for a flat fee of $497.

What is the Seeker’s Path?

An upcoming membership of monthly inquiry and practice for people finding meaning in the machine age. Join the list for founding-member access.
