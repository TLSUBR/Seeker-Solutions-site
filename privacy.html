Privacy Policy

Last updated November 13, 2026

Seeker Solutions LLC ("we", "I") runs seekersolutions.org. This page explains what information is collected, why, who helps process it, and the choices you have. The short version: I collect only what's needed to send you what you asked for, I don't sell your information, and you can leave the email list any time.

What is collected

  • Your email address, when you join the list, take the Threshold Assessment and ask for updates, request a sponsored copy, or download the free sampler.
  • Your name and message, if you use the contact form.
  • Your assessment result (for example, "The ground has shifted"), if you share your email after the Threshold Assessment. Your individual answers are not stored.
  • Where you came from: the link or website that brought you here, saved in your own browser so the form can include it. This helps me see which essays and channels are useful.
  • Purchase details, if you buy something. Payments are handled by Gumroad; I receive your name, email and what you bought, never your card details.
  • Anonymous visit statistics through Vercel Web Analytics, which counts page views without cookies and without identifying you.

How it's used

  • To deliver what you asked for: the sampler, your purchase, a reply to your message, or a sponsored copy.
  • To send the email list: a short welcome series, then occasional letters with essays, practices and offers. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link.
  • To understand which pages and channels help people, so I can improve them.

Legal bases, for visitors in the EU and UK: your consent (email list), performing a contract (purchases and sessions), and legitimate interest (basic security and anonymous statistics).

Who helps process it

These services handle information on my behalf, under their own privacy and security terms:

  • Brevo: email list and email sending
  • Gumroad: product checkout, delivery, and sales tax
  • Vercel: website hosting and anonymous analytics
  • FormSubmit: forwards contact-form messages to my inbox
  • Zoho Mail: business email
  • Cloudflare: domain and DNS

Some of these providers store data in the United States. I don't sell or rent your information, and I don't use advertising trackers.

How long it's kept

List contacts are kept until you unsubscribe or ask to be deleted. Purchase records are kept as long as tax and accounting law requires. Contact-form messages are kept as long as needed to answer you.

Your choices and rights

  • Unsubscribe with the link at the bottom of any email.
  • See, correct or delete what I hold about you by emailing travis@seekersolutions.org. I'll answer within 30 days.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California), you may have further rights, including data portability and objecting to processing. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Cookies and storage

The site doesn't set advertising or tracking cookies. It stores one small value in your browser (the page or site that first brought you here) so forms can include it. Checkout pages run by Gumroad have their own cookies and policy.

Children

This site isn't directed at children under 16, and I don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, the date at the top will change, and list members will be told by email.
