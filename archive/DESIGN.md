# The Design System: Architectural Intelligence

## 1. Overview & Creative North Star: "The Architectural Intelligence"
This design system rejects the cluttered, frantic aesthetic of typical "AI tech startups." Instead, it adopts the persona of **The Architectural Intelligence**—a digital environment that feels built with intention and structural clarity.

The Creative North Star is a fusion of high-end editorial clarity and mechanical precision. We move beyond the "template" look by utilizing intentional asymmetry, expansive whitespace, and a "Tonal Hierarchy" rooted in surface logic—not simple foreground/background contrast.
